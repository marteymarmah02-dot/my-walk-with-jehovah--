Personal study: 15-minute idea

  1. Pray for help to understand and apply what you read.
  2. Read a short Bible passage.
  3. Ask: What does this teach me about Jehovah?
  4. Ask: How can I apply this at school or at home?
  5. Write one sentence and choose one action.
